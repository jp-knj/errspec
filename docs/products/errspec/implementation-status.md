# Implementation and acceptance status

The approved plan covers the [fixed mutator 0.2.0 capability matrix](mutator-coverage.md) and existing errspec failure verification. The comparison revision is `025870640d46e93e97285d67e4c265074cf8b295`. Scope approval authorizes implementation; it does not establish that every acceptance condition has passed.

This index separates three kinds of evidence:

- **Locally validated:** implementation and committed executable regressions, including installed macOS ARM64 artifact checks where stated.
- **Remote validation:** CI starts successfully on the public repository. Check, its runner matrix, and all three Distribution platform jobs must succeed on the reviewed changes. The ARM64 jobs use the supported `macos-15` image. Exact reviewed heads, run results and artifacts are retained with the pull requests; local passes do not replace these jobs.
- **Practical evaluation:** source-free real-project observations are retained separately from fixture acceptance, product impact, and confirmed business bugs.

Full completion requires every requirement's executable acceptance, Linux x64 plus macOS ARM64/Intel installed validation, and the practical trial conditions. npm/crates publication, visibility changes, repository migration, and production deployment are outside this work. The public core/runner/typecheck packages are integrated, with source and offline installed acceptance. Their local passes do not substitute for final artifact validation on the required platforms.

## Probe reuse audit

Partial probe acquisition is a deliberate performance difference deferred under the instruction to postpone optimization. The fixed reference selects test files in [planDryRun](https://github.com/mizchi/mutator/blob/025870640d46e93e97285d67e4c265074cf8b295/packages/cli/src/coverage-cache.ts#L97-L185) and [merges fresh coverage with skipped files](https://github.com/mizchi/mutator/blob/025870640d46e93e97285d67e4c265074cf8b295/packages/cli/src/run.ts#L224-L240). Errspec reuses a whole probe only after exact-input/current-baseline validation; edits refresh all probe observations, including scope-cache mode.

The [accepted requirements](features/mutation-workflows/requirements.md) require same-input reuse, changed-input revalidation, and explicit full refresh. They do not require partial test-file acquisition or matching `dryRunFiles` telemetry. The [design](features/mutation-workflows/design.md) explicitly retains full refresh after raw changes. Cache counters prove forced refresh independently of result reuse; scope acceptance proves a fresh probe after position edits. Future optimization must preserve unknown-input fallback and acquisition validation.

## Practical interpretation and remaining completion

The source-free Journey comparison fixes both revisions, the same Vitest 5.0.3 runner, 146 unchanged existing tests, and four workers. It validates exact original slices rather than loosely matching family counts. Every one of 582 common exact edits agreed, but differing candidate inventories and one observation per timing do not establish overall equivalence or performance superiority. Native execution was slower in that observation; optimization is deferred. Raw private reports/source remain outside the repository.

The failure trial distinguishes asserted tags from source mentions, structural data-type noise, selected scope from whole-system coverage, and seeded regressions from discovered bugs. Seven missing and four mentioned values are investigation candidates, not confirmed bugs. Agent wall-clock setup/experiment timing is not human investigation cost or review-efficiency measurement.

Required remote Check/Distribution success, live Action/cache behavior, final combined package verification must be recorded before claiming full completion. Preserve useful findings/noise and review-cost limits even when all fixtures pass. The initial 30–50 person-day (6–10 week single-person with AI assistance) estimate is historical planning context, not elapsed-time evidence or a completion claim. Do not reduce unmeasured runtime/cache/runner/practical costs solely because implementation landed.
